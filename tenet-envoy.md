# Tenet Envoy

Innate **Cold** boom (**8m**, **640**). Direct hit is Impact. **28% / 2.6x / 24%.** Mag **8 / 16**. Rank **40**. ADS **guides** (slow). Hip is fast. Holster reloads **10%/s**.

**Valence: Toxin 33%** — fuse to **60%**. **Do not Vice.** Innate Cold + Toxin = **Viral** for free. Heat leftover. Overlevel to **40**.

Not a Torid. Torid is Viral + HM beam and stays Uriel / rooms. **Not Mesa.** **Not Nidus** (Synapse). **Not Oraxia.**
