# Khora

**Do not Helminth 1 or 4.** Keep Ensnare. Mod **Whipclaw** itself — parked melee does nothing for 1. Dual Ether is for swinging, not a stat stick.

## Config A — farm

Venari on **Heal**. **Pilfering Strangledome** is the loot (65%, does not scale). **Accumulating Whipclaw** is the damage. Strength is the dump. Augment caps at **+350%** additive, so **40%** Strength still hits like **390%** on 1.

**Not Melee Influence.** Chains kill outside the dome and Pilfering never sees them. **Melee Vortex** pulls into the dome.

### Stats

- Range **~250%** (Overextended + Stretch + Cunning Drift). Whip explosion caps at **200% / 10m**; the dome grab does not.
- Duration **155%** → dome **~31s**
- Efficiency **100%**. Blind Rage ruins Brief Respite.
- Strength **40%**. Leave it.

### Frame

Aura **Brief Respite**. It is **Zenurik**; her aura slot is **Vazarin**. Eat the mismatch — do not forma it. Exilus **Cunning Drift** (her Naramon).

### Play

Two domes on a choke. Whip anyone in a dome — that target takes full, everyone else in the same dome takes **50%**. Ensnare is **2x** Whipclaw; use it on acolytes. Naramon Power Spike holds combo. Park **Primary Dexterity** on a primary you do not shoot.

## Config B — melee (Influence)

No Pilfering. **No Catalyzing.** **No Overextended.** Whipclaw **Config B** + Dual Ether in the melee slot. Venari on **Attack**. Dome is a grouper, not a loot cage.

Farm dumps strength because you sit in a dome and stack Accumulating for free. Melee does not. First whip is **0 stacks**. Overextended makes that whip a wet noodle.

Aura **Growing Power**. Exilus **Cunning Drift**.

### Play

Ensnare the pack → Whip. Influence paints **20m**. Dome if you want a clump, then whip someone in it. Dual Ether on the leftovers. 2 is **2x** Whipclaw — acolytes.

Pet is **Venari Attack**. Hold **3** until the swords. **Sharpened Claws** on. Not the hound.
