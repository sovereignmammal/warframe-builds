# Soma Prime Incarnon

Slash shotgun in form. **8** pellets, **10%** crit, **3.4x**. Base form is **30% / 3x**, 200 mag, spool. Install on **Prime**, not regular. **No Galvanized Aptitude** — ignores Evolution II flat damage.

**Not** a Burston upgrade. Burston is the Heat hose. This is bleed. **Not Mesa** — Fortifying Bloodshed dumps overshields into Catalyzing.

20 weakpoints → alt-fire. Form is its own **200**-round pool. Hata-Satya **resets** when you swap form, then ramps again off the pellets.

## Riven

**Soma Toxitio** is **+109.5% Toxin** and **+105.8% Electricity** on one card. That is **~215% Corrosive**, not two leftover primaries. You cannot split Electric off, and you cannot pair the Toxin with Rime for Viral.

**Do not put it on A.** Rime + Malignant is the bleed. Park it. Cycling for Cold+Toxin is the only way this becomes Viral.
