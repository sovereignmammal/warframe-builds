# Phage

Innate **Viral** beam shotgun. **No Incarnon.** Seven beams, hip paints a room, ADS pins. **25m** — **Sinister Reach** is the exilus.

Shell Shock + Contagious combine first → **Corrosive**. **Blaze** Heat leftover. Innate Viral stays. That is the Phage card. Other guns cannot hold Viral + Corrosive + Heat at once.

**Not** a Torid. Torid is Viral + HM. **Not Mesa.** **Not Oraxia.** Do not take **A** into Anatomica. Murmur is **−50% Viral**, **+50% Electric / Radiation**, and wrapped in Overguard. Viral does nothing to Overguard. This gun will not nuke 200s — 35 damage beams. Torid is the Lab gun.
