**Titania** is **40%** Strength. Pixia base scales with Strength — Hornet and Flare are the damage cards. Viral + leftover Heat. Cascadia Flare.

Keep the Viral 60/60s **next to each other** so Heat does not fuse with Toxin into Gas.
