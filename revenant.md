# Revenant Prime — Mesmer / Reave

**2** is the frame. Charges = invuln. Recast before the last one. Companion: **Panzer**.

Mesmer charges scale with Strength (**+1** per **~16.7%**). Reave strips **8%** HP, **5×** on thralls — **250%** Strength one-shots an Enthralled target. Hit that first.

**Do not Precision Intensify.** 2 and 3 need global Strength. **Do not Catalyzing.** Mesmer is the tank. Guardian is fine.

## Play

**2 first.** Recast when charges are low, not at zero. **1** a clump → **3** through them. Do not spin **4**. Arbitration drones / acolytes / demolysts ignore Reave one-shots — gun them.

## Helminth

**Roar over Danse Macabre (4).** Keep 1 / 2 / 3. Native 4 until you have Roar. **Do not** overwrite Mesmer or Reave.

## Weapons

Whatever you already shoot. **Do not** steal Torid (Uriel), Brakk (Mesa), Dual Tox (Uriel), Synapse (Nidus). **Sun & Moon** when you swing. Phantasma is the signature — **+5** mag on this frame, not a Torid.

## Shards

**2 Amber** cast speed. Rest **Crimson Strength**. One Violet Equilibrium if Blind Rage starves you.

## Buy next
