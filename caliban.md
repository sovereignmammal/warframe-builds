# Caliban — 4 Spam (Nourish)

**Helminth:** Nourish over Gyre. Fusion Strike is hungry. Nourish already covers Viral — gun Viral is wasted (Burston **Config C**).

Companion: **Sahasa**.

Fusion Strike full-strips at **200%** strength, or **164%** with Corrosive Projection. Precision Intensify + Blind Rage puts 4 at **~289%**. Strip is capped **without** CP.

**Do not** mix Brief Respite with a fat ~650 shield pool. Catalyzing makes the pool **~60** so every 4 is a full gate.

**Do not** Augur Reach on this plan — Respite already overfills Catalyzing. Stretch is the range.

Play: **1 Nourish → 4, 4, 4.** Rank Energize and Molt Augmented.
