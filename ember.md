# Ember Prime — Heat caster

Turn Immolation on, fill the heat meter, Fire Blast for **full armor strip + Overguard**, Inferno to cook rooms. **Healing Flame** is the build.

**Do not Helminth 2/3/4.** Overwrite Fireball (1) only.

## Frame (no Helminth)

Aura **Growing Power**. Exilus **Power Drift**.

| Slot | Mod |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | **Healing Flame** |
| 2 | **Exothermic** *(Streamline if you don’t have it)* |
| 3 | Blind Rage |
| 4 | Primed Continuity |
| 5 | Primed Flow |
| 6 | Stretch *(Augur Reach over Stretch is fine)* |
| 7 | Augur Message |
| 8 | Archon Vitality |
| Arcane 1 | Molt Augmented |
| Arcane 2 | Molt Efficiency |

Stay at **100%+ Strength** so Fire Blast at a **full heat meter is 100% armor strip**. **Not** Overextended.

## Play

Immolation (2) on → Inferno (4) into crowds → when meter is **full**, Fire Blast (3). Line of sight.

## Shards

2 Amber cast speed. Rest Strength, or Topaz (secondary crit on Heat kills).

## Helminth

**Roar** or **Nourish** over Fireball (1). Next Red Veil buy after both augs: **Fireball Frenzy**.
