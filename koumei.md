# Koumei — Dice / Threads

Omamori (3) is the tank. Companion: **Panzer**. **Elusive** — **Kumihimo Loading** needs *your* kills on the buffed weapon. The fox does not steal them.

**Do not Precision Intensify.** 1 / 3 / 4 all roll the Five Fates.

Range first. Strength last. Cave uses this when it is 40+ capacity. Do not walk in on empty. Loading stays on B.

**Play:** **3** always on, recast at 5 charms. **2** on cooldown (Decree picker in Duviri). **1** on the lane. **4** dump statuses, then shoot.

Triple sixes: Omikuji skips the challenge. Omamori full invuln until those charms are gone.

## Config B — SP (Kumihimo Loading)

Loaded dice always roll **6**. **6** kills on the **passive-highlighted gun** = 1 die (**2** at 175% efficiency — do not Fleeting for that). Cap **5**. **Hold 1** spends them all.

**3** loaded dice is Shadow’s Trinity — silver threads, all **10** elemental statuses. Bank **3**, hold 1. Do not sit on 5.

**~175% Range.** 200% is the thread cap — Overextended puts them on the ceiling. Duration holds threads / charms. Streamline is the Loading tax, not Fleeting.

**Not** Overextended on B. **Not** Catalyzing. Invert tap/hold on 1 in settings if hold-cast feels wrong.

**Play:** Look at the weapon icon next to the dice. Kill with **that** one. **3** up. Bank **3** dice → **hold 1**. **4** the clump. **2** on cooldown for Decrees.

Passive can highlight an empty slot. Swap to whatever it picked.

## Helminth

**Do not** overwrite 1 or 3. 2 is Decrees, 4 is the puppet. Keep native until you are bored of Omikuji.

## Weapons

Whatever the passive lights up. **Sun & Moon** when it picks melee. Do not steal Torid / Brakk / Dual Tox / Synapse.

## Shards

**2 Amber** cast speed. Rest duration or Azure energy. Strength last — the dice multiply 1, not your mods.
