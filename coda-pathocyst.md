**Nidus** wears this. **Strain Infection** lives here, not on Sun & Moon, while this is equipped. Caustacyst is the backup scythe if this is off. Sun & Moon stays the all-frame stick.

**Valence: Electric.** Extra Electric dumps into Shocking Touch. Toxin valence dumps into Viral — keep it. **Do not Heat valence** — Shocking Touch → Radiation, Influence dies.
