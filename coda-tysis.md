# Coda Tysis

Innate **Corrosive** dart. **Heat** valence (**48%** — fuse to **60%**). **13% / 3.2x / 50%.** Semi, **2.5** fire rate, **18** mag. Rank **40**.

Dart hits IPS, then **3** Corrosive ticks over **2s**. Each tick rolls status. Multishot = more DoTs. Wall/floor splat gives the full DoT to the splash. **Punch-through is 1 tick** — do not Seeker.

**Nidus** wears this. Heat leftover is the unique — Synapse already paints Viral / Corrosive / Magnetic / Electric. **Not** Dual Tox. **Not Mesa.** **Not** Sagek. Gammacor stays parked.
