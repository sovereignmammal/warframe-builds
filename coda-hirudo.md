# Coda Hirudo — Garuda fists

Sparring. **1.25m.** **34% / 3.1x / 18%.** Puncture. Rank **40**. Crits **5%** life steal and **Invigorated** — **+5%** max HP, **15s**, **5** stacks. **+5%** move speed while held.

**Brutal Tide** matches Naramon. Grim Fury if you refuse to forma.

**Garuda** punch stick. Bloodletting dumps HP; crits put it back. **Talons stay the 4** — Blending Talons / Seeking Talons live on that config. This is the swing when you want fists. Un-Prime is junk.

**Valence: Electric or Toxin or Radiation.** Fuse **60%**. Electric dumps into Shocking Touch. Toxin leftover. Radiation leftover. **Do not Heat.** **Do not Cold.** Heat + Electric = Radiation and **Influence dies**. Cold = Magnetic.

Juno Ty already primers. **CO** is the damage card. Reach is the tax.

Does not replace Sun & Moon. Dual Ether stays Khora B. Dex stays Umbra / Voruna. Pathocyst stays Nidus. Ruvox stays Uriel. Venato stays Citrine. Amanata stays Koumei.
