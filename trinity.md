# Trinity Prime — Avenging Angel

Weapon platform + team battery. Full armor strip, huge additive crit.

## Config A — with augments (1 Forma)

Aura **Combat Discipline**. Exilus **Power Drift**.

| Slot | Mod |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Blind Rage |
| 2 | Primed Continuity |
| 3 | Narrow Minded |
| 4 | Overextended |
| 5 | Abating Link |
| 6 | Champion's Blessing |
| 7 | Vampire Leech |
| 8 | Primed Flow |
| Arcane 1 | Arcane Avenger |
| Arcane 2 | Arcane Concentration |

**Must hit 168% Strength** so Abating Link fully strips. **1 Crimson Strength shard** (or Growing Power) covers it.

Link + Blessing ≈ **94% DR**, status immunity, knockdown resist. Combat Discipline chips HP on kill → Blessing heals → Champion's Blessing stacks **+350%** additive gun crit. Avenger **+45%** flat.

## Play

Link (3) always → Blessing (4) always (recast *before* Champion's Blessing expires) → Energy Vampire (2) spam → Well of Life (1) on a tanky enemy.

Blessing heals in **affinity range**, not ability range.

## Shards

2 Amber cast speed. 1 Crimson strength. Flex more strength / duration / parkour.

## Helminth

Overwrite **Well of Life (1)** only: Silence, Roar, or Nourish. Keep 2/3/4.
