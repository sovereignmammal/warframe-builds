# Proboscis Cernos

Infested bow. Charge **0.7s**. **9m** pull, then **7m** Viral boom after **1.7s**. Direct hit is Slash/Impact. Appendages tick Slash + Viral twice. Explosion is **1003** Viral. **7% / 1.9x / 43%.** Mag **1**, ammo **7**. Silent.

Innate **Viral** on the goo. **Do not** Rime / Cryo / Malignant — Viral is glued on.

Not a Torid. Not a grouper for Khora / Nidus — they already have Ensnare / Larva. **Not Mesa.** **Not Oraxia.** Do not take it into Anatomica.
