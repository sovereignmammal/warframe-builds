# Dual Coda Torxica

Hitscan dual pistols. **25% / 2.4x / 28%.** Mag **160**. Rank **40**. Slash-weighted IPS. No innate compound.

**Valence: Heat** — fuse to **60%**. **Do not Vice.** Heat leftover is Flare. Electric / Toxin / Cold valence dump into Radiation / Gas / Blast with Heat mods.

Kill (or status kill) → **3** spores in **20m**, closest first. Guaranteed **Cold/s** + **50%** Torxica-only vuln for **20s**. Vuln double-dips DoTs like faction. One spore per enemy. Reticle grows on the glow.

Multishot is the lock: **Galvanized Diffusion + Lethal Torrent**. **Narin** hip. Spores paint Cold for 2. Not Dual Tox. **Not Mesa.** **Not** Tysis. **Not** Scyotid. Dante keeps Noctua.
