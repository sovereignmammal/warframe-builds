# Spinnerex

Oraxia’s whip. **Melee is blocked in Silken Stride** — this is for other frames, or when 4 is down.

**Trait:** hit an enemy, anything that kills them in **5s** has **10% explode chance per Toxin stack**. 480 Toxin / 10m, forced Toxin, **no crits**. Explosions ignore Condition Overload. Guns can pop it.

Viral **eats** Toxin stacks → trait **off**. Two mods that make a secondary (Blast / Magnetic / Radiation) leave innate Toxin → trait **on**.

Stance: **Coiling Viper** (matches Zenurik). Arcane: **Melee Exposure**. **Not Influence** unless you have uncombined Electric (you will not, with innate Toxin + two other mods).

**Max Galvanized Steel.** Arsenal 24% CC means it is ~rank 4. Galvanized Elementalist when you own it (replace Melee Elementalist).

## Config B — Blast + leftover Toxin (trait on)

Swap Virulent Scourge → **Volcanic Edge**. North Wind + Volcanic Edge = **Blast**. Innate Toxin stays. Unique trait on. No third element.
