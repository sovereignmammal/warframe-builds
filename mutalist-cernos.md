# Mutalist Cernos

Cloud bow. Charge **0.5s**. Arrow is **90% Impact**, **15% / 2x / 49%.** Cloud is **5** Toxin / tick, **2.5m**, **10s**, forced Toxin every second. Mag **1**, reload **0.6s**. Disposition **1.35**.

**Split Flights + Primed Firestorm** are the lock. Cloud ticks refresh Split Flights. **Do not** Galvanized Chamber — they cannot both sit. Primed Firestorm is **2.5m → 3.6m**.

Innate **Toxin** on the goo. One Cold 90 → **Viral**. **Do not** Malignant / Infected — dumps into Toxin. Heat 90 is Gas. Electric 90 is Corrosive.

Not a Torid. Not Proboscis — no pull. **Not** Dread (riven stays there). **Not Mesa.** **Not Oraxia.** Do not take it into Anatomica.
