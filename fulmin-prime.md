# Fulmin Prime — Oraxia primary

Battery rifle. **80** mag. Silken Stride extra Toxin **does** apply. Leave **Latron** on Mesa.

Innate **Electric**. Rime + Malignant combine first → **Viral**. Electric leftover. **Not** another Electric mod.

Mode swap is **1s**. Live in auto on walls. Alt-fire shotgun the Scyotid ball.
