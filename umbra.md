# Excalibur Umbra + Dex Nikana

Howl stun → swing. Steel Charge matches. Shield mods on a health-armor set are the dead slots.

## Umbra

Drop **Redirection** and **Fast Deflection**. Add **Adaptation** and **Primed Flow**. Rank the Umbral trio (set bonus is the point).

Arcanes: **Molt Augmented** + **Arcane Strike** or Energize. Not Concentration.

Howl wants **duration + range**. 145% range is acceptable. 1–2 duration shards if this is a Nikana config. Exalted Blade config wants **strength** instead.

**Loop:** 2 Howl → Dex Nikana the freeze-frames → 4 for waves.

## Dex Nikana

24% crit / 46% status. Needs Blood Rush / Weeping Wounds. Viral + Heat is the right split — do not double Toxin.

Drop: Fever Strike, Corrupt Charge, Quickening.
