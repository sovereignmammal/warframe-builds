# Burston Prime Incarnon

Aimed Heat hose. **No Galvanized Aptitude** (ignores +42 evo damage and the 2m Heat blast).

## Evolutions

- II **Forceful Finality** (all-frame). **Fortress Salvo** only if the frame has 450+ armor
- III They took **Extended Volley** (mag 66). Kinetic Battle is the recoil perk if you ever respec
- IV **Absolute Valor**

Mouths are **per-shot capped**, **status immune**, **not Deadhead headshots**. Fire rate + multishot. No Heavy Caliber. Galv Scope is wasted on mouths.

Chamber, Crit Delay, Vital Sense, **Gilded Truth**, **Vile Acceleration**, Amalgam Serration, **Bane of Infested** (Primed when you have it), Merciless.

**Do not** replace A. **Do not** put C on Hemocyte. **Do not** run C on Oraxia (Scyotid is already Corrosive — keep Burston Viral + Heat there).
