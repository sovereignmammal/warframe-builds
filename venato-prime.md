# Venato Prime — Citrine Tau scythe

**2.8m.** **34% / 2.3x / 32%.** Puncture. Any status also paints **Tau**. **10** stacks = **+100%** status chance on the target. **8s.** Influence does **not** spread Tau. Tau makes Influence Electric land more.

**Sentient Incision** is the card. **+120%** damage matching the target’s weakness. Does **not** combine — Electric stays Electric. Influence spreads that status.

**Citrine** wears this. Gem already paints Heat / Cold / Toxin / Electric. **No Viral mods. No Heat mods.** Caliban’s **+50%** combo is wasted here — he keeps Broken Scepter. Corufell is the gunblade 8 if you want Heat punches ([corufell-prime.md](corufell-prime.md)).

Does not replace Sun & Moon. Dual Ether stays Khora B. Dex stays Umbra / Voruna. Pathocyst stays Nidus. Ruvox / War Prime stay Uriel. Amanata stays Koumei. Un-Prime is junk.
