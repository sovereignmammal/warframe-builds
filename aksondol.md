# Aksondol — Narin pistols

Narin’s signatures. Hitscan duals. **24% / 2.6x / 22%.** **70** Puncture / **70** Cold. Mag **8**, reload **2.6s**. Fire rate **4**. Disposition junk.

Reload pulse: **1m** and **1** Cold proc per round spent. Empty mag = **8m**, **8** Cold. Line of sight. Instant. Centered on you when reload **starts**. Primed Fulmination and mag size grow it. **Do not** Eject Magazine — passive reload does not pulse. **Do not** cancel-reload to refresh.

Electric + Toxin fuse **Corrosive**. Innate Cold leftover. **Do not Heat** — Blast. **Do not** a lone Toxin 90 — Viral, Cold dies on the shots. **Do not** a lone Electric 90 — Magnetic.

**Narin** hip. Pulse paints Cold for 2. 3’s vulnerability stays Cold. **Sangodae pickup is fine here** — dumps into Cold. Torxica Heat + pickup is still Blast. Dual Tox stays Uriel. Brakk stays Mesa. Tysis stays Nidus. Scyotid stays Oraxia. Laetum stays Koumei. Grimoire stays Garuda.
