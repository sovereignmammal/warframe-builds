# Narin — Iceblade

**3** paints Cold. **2** drinks it. **4** is the dance — full Ice or it does not cast. Companion: **Panzer**. **Tenet Glaxion + Dual Coda Torxica.**

Ice fills when **1 / 2 / 3** hit. 4 spends the meter. **Do not Precision Intensify.** 2, 3, and 4 all want global Strength. **Do not Catalyzing.** 2 fills shields, then Overguard. Guardian is fine.

**Do not Helminth 2 / 3 / 4.** 1 is the dump.

Strength first — 2’s Overguard and 3’s Cold vulnerability scale with it. Range is 2’s siphon and 3’s landing. Duration holds 4. **45% efficiency is fine.**

Innate **V V – D**, aura **V**, exilus **–**. Put Blind Rage + Primed Continuity on the Vs, Archon Flow on the dash, Adaptation on the D. **0 forma** with Intensify. Umbral Intensify is **1 Umbra + 1 Naramon** (Equilibrium). Potato is already on. Exilus adapter for Natural Talent.

**Not** Primed Flow — Archon Flow is the same pool, and 1 / 3 / 4 actually kill with Cold. Orb is **10%** on a Cold-ability kill, **10s** CD. Guns and Cold *procs* do not count. **Not** Overextended until Strength is comfortable. **Not** Transient Fortitude — 4 dies. **Not** Archon Intensify — 2 restores shields, not HP. **Not** Archon Vitality for Heat — abilities are Cold.

Arcane **Ice Storm** when you have it: eat Adaptation. Cold stacks are the meter.

## Play

**3** into a clump (tap leap, tap land) — Cold stacks + Cold vulnerability. **2** to siphon those stacks: shields, then Overguard. More Cold on the pack = more OG. **1** a line if Ice is short. **4** when the meter is full. Attack the marks. Frozen (10 Cold) explode. 3 still works during 4. Recast 2 when shields dip, not when OG is gone.

Passive **Sangodae**: Cold stacks on kills can drop a pickup. Guns and abilities get Cold for **10s**. 3’s vulnerability eats that.

## Helminth

**Roar** or **Nourish** over **Neote (1)** once 2 + 3 feed Ice by themselves. Keep 1 until then. **Do not** overwrite Naraemagi, Hakchum, or Nurinarim.

## Weapons

**Tenet Glaxion** ([tenet-glaxion.md](tenet-glaxion.md)) + **Dual Coda Torxica** ([dual-coda-torxica.md](dual-coda-torxica.md)). 3’s vulnerability is Cold — Glaxion is the beam. Torxica spores paint extra Cold for 2. **Sangodae pickup is Glaxion’s** — on Torxica the leftover Heat + pickup Cold becomes Blast for **10s** and Flare dies. **Sun & Moon** when 4 is down. **Do not** steal Torid, Brakk, Dual Tox, Synapse, Sicarus, or Dread’s riven.
