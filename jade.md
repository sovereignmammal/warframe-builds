# Jade — Glory flyer

Two aura slots. Judgment is **+50%** damage taken for **10s**. **Glory** is the gun. **Do not Helminth.** Keep 1 (wells / heal / Archon Intensify) and 3 (strip / slow / rez).

**Do not Precision Intensify.** 2 and 3 need global Strength. Mesa is 4-only. Jade is not.

Companion: **Panzer**. Not Catalyzing on this config, so Guardian is fine.

Sheet **~220%+** Strength with Growing Power / Molt Augmented / Power of the Seven / the Archon heal proc. Eyes strip **~22%/s**. Glory DR caps **50%** at **143%** Strength — you are over. Range **~175%** (Stretch + Augur Reach). Duration holds Eyes / wells.

**Not** a second Intensify. Archon Intensify is the only one — 1 heals. **Not** Overextended. **Not** Catalyzing / Brief Respite on this 8 — the two auras are GP + CP. **Not** Energy Siphon / Nexus / Dreamer’s Bond / Vampire **in 4** — they do not tick. Orbs, Energize, Equilibrium, Adrenaline do.

Vitality doubles Glory Heat procs.

## Glory

Innate **Heat**. **15% / 2x / 20%.** Primary: **2m** Heat bombs, **10%** Judgment. Alt-fire: detonate Judgments, **4m** (**1.5×** inside a well), **25** energy, **1.5s** lock. Strength snapshots when you press **4**.

Frostbite + Pestilence combine first → **Viral**. Heat leftover.

**Alt-fire ignores** Hornet, Diffusion, fire rate, Deathbringer’s +damage, Galvanized Shot. Primary does not. Hybrid 8 anyway — you hold primary and tap alt.

## Play

Hold **2** to **Power of the Seven** → **3** Eyes → drop **1** on the choke → **4** (snapshots) → cycle **2** to **Deathbringer**. Hold primary. Alt-fire a Judged clump. Kills on Judged extend Symphony **2s**. Recast 3 while flying. Eyes rez is the Use-key replacement.
