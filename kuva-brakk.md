Magnetic progenitor (**Suvrdend Duu**, rank 36). **Do not add Cold or Electric** — that eats Magnetic.

**Cascadia Flare on Brakk and Regulators shares stacks.** Heat-click a pack, 4 comes out stacked. Dual Tox / Gammacor do not belong on this Mesa.
