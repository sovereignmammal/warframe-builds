# Latron Prime Incarnon

Range rifle. **Mesa** between Peacemakers. Do not put this on Oraxia — Incarnon form gets **0** of Silken Stride’s extra Toxin hit. That job is **Fulmin Prime**.

## Evolutions

- II **Riddled Target** (Puncture → multishot)
- III **Marksman’s Focus** (−30% zoom)
- IV **Critical Parallel** (+CC / +CD)

Caliban already strips — skip Flensing Spikes unless you take Latron on a frame that does not.
