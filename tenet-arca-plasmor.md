# Tenet Arca Plasmor

**Hahni Salvidia.** Innate **Radiation**. Infinite body punch-through. **4** ricochets. Forced **Impact**. Thick projectile. Falloff **18–36m**. **22% / 2x / 34%.** Mag **10**, reload **3s**. Heads are **1×**. Rank **40**. Disposition **0.55**.

**Valence: Toxin 51.3%** — fuse to **60%**. **Do not Vice.** One Cold 90 + Toxin valence = **Viral**. Radiation leftover.

**Arca Plasmor Acri-gelitio** — Cold **51.2%**, CD **54.8%**, Electric **47.5%**, −**24.3%** reload. Family slots. TAP disp shrinks the numbers.

The Electric is why Viral never showed. Riven Cold + Electric fuse **Magnetic** and **eat every other Cold mod**. Grasp cannot save it. **Do not** put the riven on A. Regular Plasmor is junk; do not park it there.

**Garuda** primary. Fat base. Grimoire stays the hip. Talons stay the claws. **Not** a Torid. **Not Mesa.** **Not** Glaxion (Narin). **Not** Fulmin (Oraxia). **Not** Synapse (Nidus).

## Play

Hip the lane. Bounce off the wall behind a clump for a second pass. Stay inside **18m**. Do not hold for heads.

Overlevel. Fuse Toxin toward **60%** — 51.3% is two donors, or one if the donor is **52.8%+**.
