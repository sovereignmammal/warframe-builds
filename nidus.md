# Nidus Prime — Strain / Link

Mutation tank. **No shields.** Larva groups, Virulence stacks, Link redirects, Ravenous heals. Companion: **Helminth Charger** only. **Not** the hound. **Not** Panzer.

**Strain Consume** is the frame card. Full Strain set or park the dog.

## Breakpoints

- Enemy Link **90%** damage redirect at **180%** Strength. Hit that first.
- Base Mutation cap **200**. **Abundant Mutation** → **500** (Undying gets a cooldown — Persistence covers it).
- **Parasitic Vitality** replaces any Vitality / Umbral Vitality. Health scales off Mutation while Linked.

## Play

2 Larva a clump → 1 Virulence through it (stacks + energy via Adrenaline) → **3 Link an enemy** (redirect + Vitality HP) → 4 Ravenous under your feet. Recast 1 into the carpet. Link an **ally** when you want shared Strength / weapon damage instead of redirect.

Undying spends Mutation. Keep casting.

## Helminth

**Do not** overwrite 1 / 3 / 4 on this config. Larva (2) is the only dump if you ever want **Roar** / **Nourish** for guns. Keep native Larva until the guns are bored.
