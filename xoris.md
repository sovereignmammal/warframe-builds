# Xoris — Sister hunt

Corpus glaive. Explosion is **pure Electricity**. Forced Electric on the recall boom. **Melee Influence** is the room. **Electricity must stay uncombined.**

**20% / 2.4x / 18%.** Combo duration is infinite — heavies still eat the stacks. Slowest throw of any glaive. Biggest boom (**8m** / **9m** charged). No falloff on the charged recall.

This is the **Sister / Granum** melee. Does not replace Sun & Moon. Dual Ether stays on Khora B. Dex stays Umbra / Voruna. Ruvox stays Uriel. Pathocyst stays Nidus.

**Do not park this on Khora.** Whipclaw resets Xoris combo.

## Config A — hunt (Influence heavy)

Charge-throw, detonate, Influence paints. **No Heat. No lone Fever.** Those eat Electric.

**Stance:** **Gleaming Talon** (matches Naramon). Astral Twilight if that is what you have.

**Do not** PPP and CO together. **Do not** Fever / North Wind / Volcanic Edge. **Do not** Volatile Rebound — Quick Return is the hunting card. **Do not** Blood Rush / Weeping — heavies dump combo; Corrupt Charge is the combo.

UI: **Electricity**. No Radiation. No Corrosive. Magnetic Rush in 8 is the one leftover Magnetic; Electric stays.

Xoris ignores Corrupt Charge’s duration hit. That is the exception — do not put it on Dex / Dual Ether / Sun & Moon. **+30** initial is **2x**. Ready Steel on the hunt frame is the bump if the aura slot is free. Galvanized Reflex later.

## Play

Hold melee → throw at her feet / the pack → heavy (or alt-fire) **mid-flight**. Forced Electric → Influence. Same boom kills the hound. She kneels → Parazon. Do not swing.

Galleon is the same throw. Rooms of Corpus. Stand outside your own boom or roll — it self-staggers.

**Granum hand:** Xoris must be the equipped melee to spend a Crown. Charged boom converts Errant Specters at **any** level. Do not swap this 8 for Void — the convert does not care about Corrosive.

Tenet farm. Not a general stick.
