**Do not Helminth.** Lycath is the battery, Fangs is the primer, Shroud is the crit, Ulfrun is the fun.

**Ulfrun’s Endurance** (Red Veil / Steel Meridian). **Not Prey of Dynar** on this config — that is the melee taxi.

Hold **2** (Raksh) before you fight. Hold **4** only when you want the death save.

- Strength **~250%+** (Blind Rage + Intensify + Growing Power; Molt Augmented in mission). Shroud’s +2x melee/Ulfrun crit damage scales with this.

- Duration **155%**. Lycath **~31s**, Shroud invis **~22s**.

## Play

3 up → 1 invis → 2 a clump (5 statuses × 10 stacks) → 4, click the primed one. **Wait for the bleed.** Recast 1 when invis drops. 2 during the lunge is i-frames.

**Toxic Leech cabal** (Energy Leech / Leech / Venomous): Leech puddles only drop on **visible** you. Do not shoot. Do not swing. Hold **2**. Stay in **1**. 2+4 from stealth. Ulfrun still lands in the **3.5m** Venomous cloud — that is raw Toxin, Raksh only blocks the proc. **Rolling Guard** on for this weekly (Adaptation off). Recast 1 before you ever swing. Gammacor only if Overguard will not die, from outside the green cloud, then **move** — purple puddle is 3s Magnetic, red puddle is 5% HP ticks. Catalyzing does nothing here.
