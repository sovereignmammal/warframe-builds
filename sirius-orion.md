# Sirius & Orion — Clash

Two sons, one slot. Separate 8s. **Two auras**, both live. Duplicates do not stack. Tap is the son you are. Hold swaps. Swap = **+45%** efficiency on the next **2** casts. Below **50** energy they steal from each other.

Stars from casts. Max **7**. Max **2** same color in a row. **4** is Celestial Clash — match the color. Sirius **Heat**. Orion **Slash**. Matched hits stack **+50%** CC and **+1.5x**. Invuln on the dash.

**Orion** is Primary Son — strip and group first. **Do not Helminth.** Hold/tap eats the swap. Infusing 4 kills Clash on **both**. **Do not Precision Intensify.** Both sons and Clash want global Strength.

Companion: **Panzer**. Catalyzing is the gate — no Guardian.

Gravitic Slash full-strips at **200%** Strength (**164%** with CP). Light’s Sanctuary **75%** DR cap at **167%**.

Orion: **2 → 3 → 1**. Swap. Sirius: **3** the well → **2** motes → **1** pickups. Stars hit 7. **4**. Match green to Sirius (fire), red to Orion (aim). Mismatch dims the stack. Cancel 4 if you panic — stars stay.
