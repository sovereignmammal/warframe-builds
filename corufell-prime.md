# Corufell Prime — Citrine gunblade scythe

Heavy Scythe. Heavy becomes a **Heat** gunblade. Two shots. Infinite punch-through. Falloff **15–30m**. Second shot **does not eat combo**. Prime vines are the faster transform. **Citrine +20%** wind-up.

**26% / 2.4x / 14%.** Mostly Impact on lights. **Electricity must stay uncombined.** Innate Heat on the projectile is leftover.

**Citrine only** as a gunblade. Venato Prime is her Influence lights ([venato-prime.md](venato-prime.md)). Does not replace Sun & Moon. Dual Ether stays Khora B. Dex stays Umbra / Voruna. Pathocyst stays Nidus. Ruvox / War Prime stay Uriel. Amanata stays Koumei. Un-Prime is junk.

## Config A — Influence heavy (Citrine)

Gem already paints four statuses. **CO** is the damage card. **Do not** PPP with it. **No Heat mods** — Radiation eats Influence. Focus Energy is Electric, it stacks with Shocking Touch.

**Stance:** **Galeforce Dawn** *(matches Madurai)*.

Reflex + Focus = **90%** efficiency cap. Amalgam Organ Shatter **+60%** wind-up, Killing Blow another **+60%**, Citrine **+20%**.

**Do not** Fever / North Wind / Volcanic Edge. **Do not** PPP and CO together. **Do not** Galvanized Reflex — it eats Reflex Coil. **Do not** Weeping — 14% SC, gem is the primer.

PPP in the CO hole only if the gem is down. Then the gunblade is a stick, not this 8.

## Play

**3** on Vizier. **2** up. Lights to **4** → Tennokai heavy → **two** Heat punches. Influence paints off Electric. Hold heavy between Tennokai; 90% efficiency keeps the multiplier. Bullet-jump during wind-up if you need air.

**Do not** slide-heavy — that is a normal scythe swing, no gunblade. **Do not** slam. Recast 3 before the gem dies.

- Put Heat on it.
