**Do not Viral.** Murmur is **−50% Viral**, **+50% Electric / Radiation**. Overguard does not take Viral. Dual Tox stays the general kill pistol. **Not Mesa** — Brakk owns that hip.

Convulsion + Heated Charge combine first → **Radiation**. No leftover Heat, so **no Cascadia Flare**.
