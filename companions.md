### Akaten (weapon)

Base range is junk. **Reach is why it misses.** **Not** Blood Rush. **Not** Fury — Elementalist is the speed card.

Live loadout landed **Viral + Radiation** (Cold+Toxin, Heat+Electric). Puncture/Slash leftover. Correct primer — leave it.

Recommended skeleton if you rebuild: Reach, Melee Elementalist, Shocking Touch, Primed Fever Strike, Vicious Frost, Volcanic Edge, Melee Prowess, Primed Pressure Point. Frost + Fever = Viral, Electric leftover, Heat leftover.
