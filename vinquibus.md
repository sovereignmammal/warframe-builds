**40% / 3.2x / 20%.** Puncture. Mag **8**. Reload **3s**. Range **3m**. Disposition junk. **Not** DA / Duviri.

Headshots: **2** stacks Precision, max **16**, **+250%** melee. Multishot and punch-through make more stacks. One connect eats **one** stack.

Melee kills: **4** stacks Ferocity, max **16**, **100%** ammo efficiency. One shot eats **one** stack, hit or miss. That is the reload.

**Sirius & Orion.** Occupies primary **and** melee. Hip is free. Uriel keeps Torid + Ruvox. Does not replace Sun & Moon on other frames. Pride / Wrath have no bonus — this is the stick.
