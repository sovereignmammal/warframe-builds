# Boltor Prime Incarnon

Worse Burston. Variety rifle. Adapter on **Prime**, not regular.

Slash-weighted pseudo-shotgun. **3** base multishot, **160** Incarnon mag. Charge **20** weakpoints.

**Crimson Overture is +0 damage on Prime.** Mantra is the card.

## Evolutions

- II **Hunter’s Mantra**
- III **Rapid Reinforcement**
- IV **Commodore’s Fortune**

**Galvanized Aptitude works here.** It does not on Burston.

Rime + Malignant = Viral. Heat leftover.
