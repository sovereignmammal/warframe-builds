# Frost Prime — Avalanche

Freeze, strip, shoot. Ice Wave is the dump slot. Duration barely matters.

**Avalanche full-strips at 167% strength, or 137% with Corrosive Projection.**

**Do not** use Archon Intensify for a heal proc — Frost does not restore health. Icy Avalanche is Overguard. **Do not** use Archon Vitality for Heat — Frost abilities are Cold (still use it as the HP card).
