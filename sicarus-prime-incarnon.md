Incarnon is the weapon. Charge weakpoints (**12** hits / **4** bursts), alt-fire. Ricochets once within **6m**. **120** Incarnon rounds. Ricochets can hit weakpoints — **not reliably**.

Not Dual Tox. **Not Mesa.** **Not** Tysis. **Not** Torxica. Pair with Dual Ether; this pistol does not need a primer.
