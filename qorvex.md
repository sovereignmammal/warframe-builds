# Qorvex — Crucible Blast

Dump duration, stack strength/range. Keep **Crucible Blast**. Do not Helminth over 4 on this config.

**227% strength** clears Wrecking Wall strip. **175% range** makes Crucible chains connect. **40% duration** is the trade — recast 1 on cooldown, or **Fused Crucible** so pillar timers freeze while 4 is on.

## Frame

Blind Rage + Fleeting Expertise + Streamline. Catalyzing Shields + armor. Aura **Corrosive Projection** if you gun.

**Catalyzing without Brief Respite** is a weak gate — either accept armor + Disometric Guard, or put Brief Respite on a second config.

Umbral Intensify unmatched is tax. Forma to Umbra, or **Archon Intensify** if you heal.

**Play:** 1 → 2 strip → 4 through the pack. Rank Molt Augmented and Energize. Amber shards for 4 windup, not Natural Talent in leftover capacity.
